Instructional design methodology

The Beat Typology

Ten instructional functions a block of a technical lesson can perform, each defined by what it does to the learner rather than by what it looks like on screen. Choose the function first. The interaction is the last decision, not the first.

The same ten types, six subjects

The typology is defined by instructional function, so it does not change when the subject does. What changes is the instantiation. This is the whole claim, laid out in one grid.

The electrical column carries the most entries marked built because those lessons were constructed first, not because the method is electrical. The applied types in particular are frequently misread as requiring a physical system. They do not. They require a rule-governed model whose state the learner changes and whose response is computed. Information lost across a shift handover is such a model.

    06

    The governing rules

    Six constraints that hold in every lesson, in every domain. Each states what it prevents, because a rule whose cost is invisible is the first one dropped.

    Function before interaction

    The instructional function is chosen from the objective. The interaction is chosen from the subject. The build comes last.

    Prevents: a lesson designed around an available component, which satisfies every surface rule and teaches the wrong thing well.

    Prediction precedes manipulation

    On an applied beat the learner commits to an outcome first, and that commitment drives the model.

    Prevents: exploration in place of instruction. The learning sits in the gap between what was predicted and what occurred, and a control operated without commitment closes no gap.

    The prose carries the model

    Definition and Explanation together account for roughly half the learner-visible words. They are not connective material between the interactions.

    Prevents: learners who can operate the controls and cannot explain the system. For low prior knowledge readers, more explicit causal text produces better comprehension, not less.

    The wrong path stays reachable

    On a Procedure beat the incorrect action can be taken and its consequence is shown.

    Prevents: a demonstration wearing an interface. An error committed and immediately corrected retains better than an error prevented.

    Nothing is fabricated

    Measured values, indications, citations and quotations come from source documentation. Where a source is silent the build stops and asks.

    Prevents: a plausible number entering a training library and being taught as fact. This constraint outranks completeness, schedule, and the appearance of finish.

    One function per beat

    A beat performs exactly one instructional function and controls exactly one gate.

    Prevents: two interactions competing for the same working memory, where neither is consolidated. Also makes the lesson auditable, since every beat can be checked against the objective it claims.

    07

    What this methodology does not yet establish

    Stated plainly, because a design standard that hides its limits invites the wrong kind of confidence.

    Open questionCurrent position
    Learning evidenceThe typology has been validated against expert judgment and internal consistency, not against learner outcomes. No lesson built to this standard has been compared with one that was not on any measure of retention or transfer.
    ExhaustivenessThe ten types were abstracted from one mature course and cross-checked against Gagné and Merrill. That produces a working catalog, open to extension. It does not establish that a eleventh type is impossible.
    Category boundariesPractice and Procedure both admit ordered tasks and require a discriminating criterion. The working boundary is whether the action changes system state.
    Phase orderingStated as a default with rationale, not as a law. Productive failure research supports attempting before instruction in some conditions, which would place an applied beat before Explanation.
    SpacingThe unit of analysis is one lesson. Retention across weeks is the largest single effect in the supporting literature and is out of scope here, which means the typology is incomplete as a curriculum method.
    ExpertiseThe design assumes a first-exposure learner. For experienced technicians, heavy Definition and Demonstration are counterproductive by the same literature that justifies them for novices.

    The claim this methodology makes is narrow and it is worth stating exactly. It is that a lesson whose every block performs a named instructional function, chosen from the objective and built to a stated requirement, is more defensible and more consistent than one assembled from whatever interactions were available. That claim is supportable today. The stronger claim, that lessons built this way teach better, requires evidence that has not been gathered, and until it is, the honest register is a house standard rather than a finding.
