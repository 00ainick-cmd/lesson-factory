## Keep (Nick)

Resistance is the opposition to the flow (current) of electricity. Unit: Ohms. Symbol: R.

There are 2 types: fixed has a set value, variable has an adjustable value.

Series: Rt = R1 + R2 + Rn. If you remove a resistor in series, total resistance decreases.

Parallel: reciprocal of the sum of the individual reciprocals. If you remove a resistor in parallel, total resistance increases.

MAKE SURE YOU ISOLATE THE RESISTOR YOU ARE TRYING TO MEASURE.

Gold and Silver are NEVER the first band.

Open: infinite. Short: very little. Closed switch: close to 0 ohms. Wire: .2-.3 ohms is a good reading.

We use copper for aircraft wire because it is cheap and conducts well.
