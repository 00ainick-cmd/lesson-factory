The unit has a name Ohm is not a brand. Georg Simon Ohm, locksmith's son, published the law in 1827. The 1825 paper is wire length versus force, not the law. German physics ignored the math for 14 years. Copley Medal, 1841. University chair, Munich, 1852, two years before he died. The ohm as a practical unit was adopted in 1881, 27 years after he died. Full timeline, fun facts, and the three-form sandbox live in Ohm's Law (C07). This lesson is the quantity: opposition, series, parallel, color, and the ohmmeter.

Inside the metal

Electrons bouncing off atoms

A conductor has many free electrons. They bounce off atoms as they move, and that bouncing is the opposition we call resistance. Heat makes the atoms shake more, so there are more collisions. Alloys put the wrong-size atoms in the way, which also increases resistance. Change the material from copper to nichrome, or raise the temperature, and you will see scattering go up and the electrons slow down. That is the same physics the bench is showing in FIG 2.

Series and parallel

Series adds. Parallel is the reciprocal.

Add a resistor. Pull one out. Watch total R. Series is 1 path, so pulling a part takes resistance out of the only path and total goes down. Parallel is more than 1 path, so pulling a part takes a path away and total goes up.

Series · one path

Rt = R1 + R2 + Rn
60 Ω total

Start with 10 + 20 + 30. Remove one and total resistance decreases.

Parallel · more than 1 path

reciprocal of the sum of the individual reciprocals
3.33 Ω total

Three 10 Ω paths. Remove one path and total resistance increases.

Back to the Cirrus Corroded joint = extra R in series with the fan. Total resistance goes up. Current goes down. Fan starves. Series adds. That added R is the fault.

Add or remove a resistor. Watch total R go up or down.

Color code

Each color is a digit from 0 to 9

A 4-band resistor is a number written in color. Band 1 is the first digit. Band 2 is the second digit. Band 3 uses those same colors as the number of zeros to write after those two digits. Band 4 is tolerance. Gold and Silver are NEVER the first band. They are only the fourth. Example: Red, Orange, Blue, Silver = 23,000,000 +/- 10%. Red is 2, orange is 3, blue is 6 zeros.
