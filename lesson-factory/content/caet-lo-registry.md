## 1. Regulatory Framework

- **1.1** Classify maintenance, preventive maintenance, and major versus minor alterations under FAR Part 43 and Appendix A.
  Study guide: FAR Part 43 - Maintenance Authority; STCs and ICAs. Bank items: 7.

- **1.2** Document maintenance, inspections, and test results in maintenance records as required by FAR 43.9, 91.411, and 91.413.
  Study guide: FAR Part 43 (return to service); Documentation Requirements. Bank items: 3.

- **1.3** Identify required inspection intervals and authorized personnel for pitot-static and transponder inspections under FAR 91.411 and 91.413.
  Study guide: FAR Part 91 - Inspection Requirements. Bank items: 3.

- **1.4** Explain Part 145 repair station requirements: ratings, capability lists, current technical data, and personnel training and testing programs.
  Study guide: FAR Part 145 - Repair Stations. Bank items: 6.

- **1.5** Identify the purpose of certification and approval documents including STCs and FAA Form 8130-3.
  Study guide: STCs and ICAs. Bank items: 2.

## 2. Electrical Theory

- **2.1** State Ohm's Law and select the correct form of the equation for a given unknown.
  Study guide: Ohm's Law. Bank items: 2.

- **2.2** Calculate voltage, current, resistance, and power in DC circuits using Ohm's Law and the power formulas.
  Study guide: Ohm's Law; Power Calculations. Bank items: 4.

- **2.3** Predict how circuit current changes when voltage or resistance changes.
  Study guide: Ohm's Law. Bank items: 2.

- **2.4** Determine total resistance and total current in series and parallel circuits and identify circuit type from observed behavior.
  Study guide: Series Circuits; Parallel Circuits. Bank items: 5.

- **2.5** Describe AC fundamentals: waveform, frequency, transformers, and the advantages of AC systems.
  Study guide: AC vs DC; Power Conversion Devices. Bank items: 4.

- **2.6** Describe the function of electrical system components: battery, bus, voltage regulator, circuit protection, and capacitors.
  Study guide: Fundamental Concepts; Circuit Protection. Bank items: 8.

- **2.7** Troubleshoot electrical faults including abnormal voltage drop, dim or fluctuating output, and parasitic current draw.
  Study guide: (not in current study guide). Bank items: 7.
